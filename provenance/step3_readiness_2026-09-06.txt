Pre-outcome audit result
------------------------
The initially proposed local-Gaussian oracle is based on marginal-composite
population moments. It does not reproduce the package's integrated
respondent-sequence fit, nested cross-fitting, target-specific empirical Riesz
solves, outer-fold diagnostics, or common respondent multiplier procedure.
It is therefore a geometry/face-validity surrogate and cannot authorize a
production calibration.

The actual-fit numerical runner now calls the package's q=1 full and nested
fit path and has separate ordinary and transport contracts. Its signed
readiness check remains false because nine required end-to-end adapters are
not implemented. These include target recomputation, level-B target and
influence evaluation, repolishing, derivative coordinates, known-rank
benchmarks, multiplier linkage, registered fault fits, q=2 sentinels, and the
joint F008 selector.

An installed-package check found one additional portability error before the
branch was shared. The truth-manifest validator originally looked only for
source-tree R files, which are not present at those paths after package
installation. The package now ships immutable audit copies of the three
truth-generation source files under inst/calibration/v23/source, checks their
SHA-256 values against the prospective truth manifest, and tests that the
copies equal the source-tree files. This repair changes no truth, outcome, or
calibration constant.

Corrections made before any frozen-design outcome
-------------------------------------------------
- The subgroup split uses the first respondent-varying response-free moderator
  centered at its empirical mean. High is z > 0 and low is z <= 0. This keeps
  the Saha-Weeks binary moderator instead of creating an empty subgroup.
- The mean MRS target is -numerator/denominator, matching the paper.
- Scalar chart and nonlinear composite diagnostics have distinct consumers.
- G004 is a fit-level q=1 active-eigenvalue diagnostic and is not a substitute
  for target-direction residual variance.
- Preference-direction and compensating-share targets do not inherit the
  directional-heterogeneity primitive gates.
- Unsafe severity labels are target specific. In particular, shrinking the
  residual loading while leaving explained heterogeneity positive does not
  create an unsafe cell for the total-heterogeneity diagnostic.
